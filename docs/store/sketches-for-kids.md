**Description (EN)**

Sketches for kids! is a box of six toys for little hands, with nothing to read and nothing to buy by accident.

• The sea: tilt the phone and the water sloshes; a little boat bobs and slides, and putters like a tin motorboat when you hold it. Tap the water for a splash, tap the sun to make it giggle, drag it down for a sunset and a starry night.
• Paint: paint with your fingers in thick, glossy paint that mixes like the real thing (blue and yellow make green), with the soft sound of a brush on paper.
• Shapes: tap the coloured shapes and each one plays its instrument: marimba, bells, kalimba.
• Animals: ten friendly animals; tap one to hear its name, then its cry.
• Guitar: strum a real recorded nylon guitar, pick a chord, play a tune up the neck.
• Parrot: say something, stop, and the parrot repeats it in a silly voice.

The app speaks English, French, Spanish and Italian. The sea and the animals are free. One purchase, "All toys", unlocks the other four, forever, behind a question for parents. No ads, no accounts, no data collected. The parrot listens on the phone only; nothing is recorded or sent.

To leave a toy, tap the house sticker. Parents: the gate (a sum) opens the settings, where any toy can be hidden.

**Review notes (EN, paste in « Notes »)**

Made for kids, age 5 and under. No account, no network, no third-party SDK, no analytics, no ads.
Parental gate: the person sticker (top right of the home screen) and every locked toy ask a sum of two
numbers written in words in the device language (e.g. "What is seven plus five?"); type the answer on
the keypad. The gate precedes the in-app purchase, the settings and the two links (privacy policy,
support e-mail). The in-app purchase "All toys" (com.screenclub.sketchesforkids.alltoys) unlocks Paint,
Shapes, Guitar and Parrot; restore is on the same page. Microphone: used only by the Parrot toy, on
device, to repeat the child's phrase in a pitched voice; audio stays in memory for a few seconds and is
never stored or transmitted; an ear sticker pulses while the microphone is on. To leave a toy, tap the
house sticker at the top left. The app is localized in English, French, Spanish and Italian.
